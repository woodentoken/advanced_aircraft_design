## Mission Requirements

#### SAR specifics

**Parameter Value / Assumption**
Home base / U.S. Coast Guard Air Station San Francisco
Outbound mission / radius 800 nmi (return to base after loiter)
Loiter requirements / 2.5 hours at 10,000–20,000 ft
Payload / 10 crew + sensors + rescue gear
Certification / Type 14 CFR Part 25

#### Assumptions

Assume ISA conditions, fuel reserves of 5% + 45-min hold at 1,500 ft, and taxi fuel for 15 min.
We are primarily working in an over-water search and rescue scenario.

#### Sustainability Target

Your design must achieve _at least_ a 15% reduction in fuel burn or lifecycle CO₂ emissions per
mission, relative to the baseline aircraft.
